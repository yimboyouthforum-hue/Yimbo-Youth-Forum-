About us

A common space for Yimbo's young people to grow

Yimbo Youth Forum is a platform created to unite young people in Yimbo and provide a common space where they can grow, support one another, access opportunities, develop their talents, and participate meaningfully in community development.

Our vision

A united, empowered, informed and prosperous youth community actively contributing to the development of Yimbo and beyond.

Our mission

To empower and unite the youth of Yimbo through opportunities, skills development, networking, leadership, entrepreneurship, talent development and community engagement.

Our values

Unity Integrity Accountability Inclusivity Innovation Leadership Service
